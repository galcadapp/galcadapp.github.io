Wax ma kaa xanniban yihiin?

Jawaabaha su’aalaha rakibidda.

Sideen u rakibaa app-ka?

Marka soo dejintu dhammaato, taabo Open ee browser-ka ama ogeysiiska telefoonka. Kadib taabo Install. Marka rakibiddu dhammaato, taabo Open si aad u furto Galcad.

Android wuxuu i weydiiyay oggolaansho

Haddii aad ku kalsoon tahay app-ka iyo halka aad ka soo dejisay, fur Settings marka lagu weydiiyo, kadib daar “Allow from this source”. Ku noqo rakibidda. Oggolaanshaha waad damin kartaa markaad dhammayso. Haddii digniin amni soo baxdo, akhri ka hor intaadan sii wadin.

Xagee ka helaa faylka la soo dejiyay?

Fur Chrome ⋮ → Downloads ama app-ka Files → Downloads. Dooro galcad-app.apk si aad u bilowdo rakibidda.

Soo dejintu ma bilaabmayso

Hubi internet-kaaga oo boggan ku fur browser-ka telefoonkaaga. Isku day mar kale, ama fur bogga soo dejinta.
