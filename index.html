Maxaan u arki waayay progress %?

Si download-ku u noqdo mid fudud oo browser-ku maamulo, boggu ma isticmaalo JavaScript si uu faylka u dhex mariyo memory-ga. Progress-ka dhabta ah ka eeg qaybta Downloads ee Chrome.

Haddii Android yiraahdo "Install unknown apps"?

Kaliya haddii aad ku kalsoon tahay APK-ga iyo isha uu ka yimid, Android Settings ka oggolow browser-ka inuu rakibo app-ka. Rakibidda kadib oggolaanshaha waad ka laaban kartaa.

Haddii Play Protect uu APK-ga xannibo?

Ha damin Play Protect ama digniintiisa si indho la'aan ah ha u dhaafin. Marka hore xaqiiji in APK-gu yahay kii saxda ahaa, hubi checksum-kiisa, kadibna la xiriir soo saaraha haddii digniintu sii jirto.

Sidee u xaqiijiyaa faylka v1.0?

GitHub wuxuu ku daabacay SHA-256-kan APK-ga v1.0. Wuxuu khuseeyaa v1.0 oo keliya; haddii latest la cusboonaysiiyo, checksum cusub ayaa loo baahan yahay.
