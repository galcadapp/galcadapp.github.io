Soo deji Galcad

Marka soo dejintu dhammaato, taabo Open ee fariinta Chrome.

Open → Install → Open

Fur faylka → Rakib → Fur Galcad

Sida loo rakibo Galcad

  1. 1
    Taabo “Soo deji Galcad”

    Haddii Chrome ku tuso “File might be harmful”, taabo “Download anyway” oo keliya haddii aad ku kalsoon tahay app-ka iyo boggan.

  2. 2
    Markuu dhammaado, taabo “Open”

    Taabo Open ee fariinta Chrome. Haddii fariintu qarsoonto, shaashadda korka hoos uga jiid oo taabo ogeysiiska soo dejinta Galcad.

  3. 3
    Taabo “Install”, kadib “Open”

    Sug inta app-ka la rakibayo. Kadib taabo Open si aad u furto Galcad.

Telefoonku wuxuu codsaday oggolaansho

Haddii aad ku kalsoon tahay Galcad, taabo Settings, daarna Allow from this source. Dib ugu noqo oo taabo Install. Oggolaanshaha waad damin kartaa marka rakibiddu dhammaato.

Ma arko “Open”

Fur Chrome, taabo saddexda dhibcood ⋮, dooro Downloads, kadib taabo galcad-app.apk.

Soo dejintu ma bilaaban

Boggan ku fur Chrome oo hubi internet-kaaga. Kadib mar kale taabo Soo deji Galcad.
